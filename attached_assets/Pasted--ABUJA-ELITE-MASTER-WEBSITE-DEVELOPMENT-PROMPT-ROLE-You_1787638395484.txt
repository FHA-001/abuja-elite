# ABUJA ELITE — MASTER WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

## ROLE

You are the lead product designer, senior frontend engineer, UI/UX designer, backend architect, and technical project manager responsible for building a premium website for **Abuja Elite**.

This is a real production project, not a generic template website.

The objective is to create a website that accurately communicates the identity, exclusivity, sophistication, community and journey of Abuja Elite while remaining technically maintainable, portable, responsive and ready for future content management.

You must work **phase-by-phase**.

DO NOT attempt to build the entire application at once.

DO NOT implement future phases before they are approved.

Before beginning each phase, explain what you intend to build, the relevant design/technical decisions, and the files/components that will be affected. Wait for approval before moving to the next major phase.

Abuja Elite is NOT primarily a clothing brand.

The T-shirts associated with Abuja Elite are presented to members as a symbol of belonging to the Abuja Elite community. They should therefore never be treated as normal merchandise or an e-commerce product unless this is explicitly introduced in a future phase.

Abuja Elite is a community/platform centered around:

* High-profile collaborations
* Promising and upcoming entrepreneurs
* Successful and ambitious people connected to Abuja
* Luxury businesses and personalities
* Modern success stories
* Consistency
* Hard work
* Excellence
* Luxury lifestyle
* Community
* Networking
* The journey and achievements of people within Abuja

Abuja Elite frequently highlights and collaborates with prominent luxury businesses and people in Abuja.

Examples of recent additions/collaborations mentioned by the client include:

* Manga Automobiles
* Abana Cars

These examples may be represented on the website where appropriate, but DO NOT invent facts, biographies, achievements, partnerships, quotes, statistics or claims about any person or company.

The website should communicate the concept of:

**People. Ambition. Excellence. Community. Abuja.**

The existing brand logo should be respected and used prominently.

The supplied logo contains the Abuja Elite emblem, the words:

**THE ELITE**

and the existing tagline:

**LIVING LIFE AT THE TOP, WHERE I BELONG.**

The client has supplied the logo asset separately and it should be used as the primary brand mark.

# 4. VISUAL DIRECTION

The visual identity should combine:

### DARK LUXURY

with

### BRIGHT, LUMINOUS CLARITY.

This is NOT a light/dark mode system.

There should be ONE primary visual identity.

The site should have a dark foundation while still feeling bright, clear, spacious and visually alive.

Use:

* Black
* Deep charcoal
* Warm off-white
* Soft ivory
* Champagne/gold
* Subtle metallic-gold accents
* Controlled luminous effects

Avoid excessive gold.

Gold should feel refined and premium rather than loud.

The site should use strong contrast so all text remains highly readable.

Do not make the entire website pure black.

Use different shades and surfaces to create depth.

Possible visual hierarchy:

* Deep black hero sections
* Charcoal content sections
* Warm off-white text
* Gold accents
* Bright photography
* Soft gold/white glows
* Light surfaces where they improve readability and visual rhythm

The result should feel:

**dark + bright + premium + smooth.**
